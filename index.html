    Send invoice

    Choose how you want to share the PDF with your client.

    Share PDF

    Opens your phone’s share sheet. Choose WhatsApp, email or another app to share the PDF.

    WhatsApp

    Opens a chat with the client’s phone number and prepares the message. Attach the downloaded PDF if prompted.

    Email

    Downloads the PDF and opens a draft to the client. The signature uses shelricktech@gmail.com; choose that account as the sender in your email app and attach the PDF.

    WhatsApp and email require your confirmation before sending. The business WhatsApp shown on invoices is +254 721 752 212. If your phone supports file sharing, Share PDF lets you attach the invoice directly.

    Install Shelrick Invoice App

    Install it once to open it like an app and use it offline.

    1. Android / Chrome: open this app’s secure HTTPS link, tap Install app (or ⋮ → Install app / Add to Home screen).
    2. iPhone / Safari: open the secure HTTPS link, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
    3. After installation, saved clients, settings and invoices remain on that phone. They are not uploaded to a server.
    A PWA must be hosted over HTTPS to install and cache offline. See the included README for the quick Vercel deployment steps.
